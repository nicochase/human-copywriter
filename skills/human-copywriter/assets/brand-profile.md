# Brand profile template

Create a separate private profile per brand when requested. Replace the guidance below with verified/supplied facts; leave unknowns explicit. Do not use this template as factual input.

## Identity and reader
Brand/site, audience, reader tasks, offer and desired next actions.

## Product/service facts
What is available now; scope, differentiators, limitations, actual pricing/terms and dates for changing information. Identify facts supplied by the owner versus independently checked.

## Proof
Approved claims, source/locator, exact quotes, usage restrictions, date and population/scope. Include what must not be claimed.

## Voice
Authentic samples and their sources; vocabulary/register; rhythm; point of view; humor; typography; differences between articles and commercial pages. Separate observed habits from requested preferences.

## Website conventions
Output format, meaningful headings/anchors, existing internal destinations and any exact terms that must be retained.

## Open questions and maintenance
Missing proof, unresolved facts, last-reviewed date and user corrections. Keep this profile outside the public skill repository.
